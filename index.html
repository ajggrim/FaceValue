What could go wrong, and how Tender handles it
Misread characters

Cameras confuse O/0, I/1, S/5, B/8. We fix likely swaps, flag every one we changed, and always let you edit before checking.

Impossible serials

Real serials start at 00000001, the prefix is a Federal Reserve letter A–L, and the format is fixed. Anything else fails validation instead of getting a price.

Altered or forged “fancy” numbers

Fancy serials are the ones people fake, by changing digits or printing fake bills. Any bill scoring high gets a warning to verify it in person before paying a premium.

Duplicate scans

The same serial and series twice in one stack is flagged, since a genuine note only exists once per series and bank.

Overpromising on price

We show a range, not a single number, and say the figures come from a simple sample model. Real prices depend on the market and a professional grade.
